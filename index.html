1.

When your system is running on empty,
don't ask it to do more.

Cut what's draining you.
Add back what's holding you up.

Less noise. Less input. Less explaining. Less performing. Less deciding.

More food. More fluids. More sleep. More darkness. More quiet. More support.

You don't have to be functional today.

You just have to stop making it harder for your body to recover.

For the next 24 to 72 hours, your job is not to fix your life.

1
Stop adding stimulation. Phone down. Lights dim. Noise off.
2
Get water and real food into your body. Regular meals: protein and carbs, not just coffee.
3
Sleep and rest more than you normally would, if your body's asking for it. It's asking for it.
4
Cancel what can be cancelled: work, plans, errands, the conversations that can wait.
5
Tell at least one safe person what's actually happening.
6
Turn down the sensory input. Darker, quieter, softer, whatever temperature actually feels okay.
7
Don't make big decisions from empty. They can wait until you're not.
If you're genuinely unwell, suddenly worse, unable to eat or drink, fainting, chest pain, trouble breathing, confusion, anything that scares you, that's not nervous-system overload. Get medical care. This kit isn't the right tool for that.
